Guitar sound (assets/guitar/*.mp3)

Steel-string acoustic guitar notes from the "FluidR3_GM" General MIDI SoundFont
by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered MP3 files by the MIDI.js Soundfonts project
(Benjamin Gleitzman): https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes made for Gitara: files renamed by MIDI note number (40 = E2 ... 86 = D6).
The audio itself is unchanged; the app corrects each note's pitch by a few cents
during playback so tuner reference tones are exact.
